# Pricing change 2026 (internal announcement, 12 January 2026)

From 1 February 2026, list prices go up by 18% for new customers. Existing customers move to the new price at their next renewal.

| Plan | Old list price | New list price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter (self-serve, up to 49 employees) | EUR 129 per month (annual) | EUR 152 per month (annual) |
| Growth (self-serve, up to 49 employees) | EUR 399 per month (annual) | EUR 471 per month (annual) |
| Business (sales-led, 50 to 999 employees) | from EUR 9,600 per year | from EUR 11,300 per year |
| Enterprise (1,000+ employees) | custom | custom |

From 15 January, the pricing page no longer lets companies with 50 or more employees buy self-serve. They see "Talk to sales" and go to the demo form.
