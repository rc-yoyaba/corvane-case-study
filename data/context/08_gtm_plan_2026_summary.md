# GTM plan 2026: summary (approved December 2025)

Prepared by Mia Lorenz (RevOps) and Daniel Reed (Marketing) for the 2026 budget, before the CRO hire.

| Item | 2026 plan |
|---|---|
| Closing ARR, December 2026 | EUR 45.5M |
| New ARR, Mid-Market | EUR 5.6M |
| New ARR, Enterprise | EUR 3.0M |
| New ARR, Self-serve | EUR 2.1M |
| New ARR, Partner | EUR 0.5M |
| MQLs per quarter | 1,200 |
| MQL to SQL | 30% |
| SQL to Opportunity | 75% |
| Win rate (Opportunity to Won) | 28% |
| AEs | 10 (DACH quota EUR 420k, US quota USD 480k, Enterprise EUR 900k) |
| SDR target | 45 SQLs per quarter each |

Initiatives behind the plan:
1. Move the AE threshold from 200 to 50 employees (January) to give AEs more pipeline.
2. Raise list prices by 18% from 1 February. Existing customers move at their renewal date.
3. Launch the LedgerPoint partner programme (Q2).
4. Scale Enterprise with a third Enterprise AE.
5. Triple MQL volume through paid, content and events.

Math used for the MQL target: 4 quarters x 1,200 MQLs x 30% x 75% x 28% = about 300 new deals, at an average ACV of EUR 35k = EUR 10.5M new ARR.
